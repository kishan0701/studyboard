Point 3 Problem Statement Core Challenge
🗣️ What to speak: "Ma'am/Sir, in our college routine, students receive academic tasks across scattered channels: informal WhatsApp group chats where circulars get lost, physical notice boards, and verbal in-class instructions like 'Bring your OS Lab 4 journal tomorrow'. Students fail to prioritize urgency, leading to missed practical deadlines and internal evaluation loss."
  • Information Fragmentation: WhatsApp messages lack searchability and calendar synchronization.
  • High Deadline Failure Rate: No automated visual urgency alert system exists for upcoming submissions.
  • Lack of Real-time In-Class Reminders: Verbal faculty instructions are rarely tracked systematically.
Point 4 Objectives of the Project Measurable Goals
  • Centralize All Coursework: A single responsive portal for both official circulars and student assignments.
  • Automated Deadline Risk Engine: Categorize pending tasks into 5 dynamic states (DONE, OVERDUE, HIGH RISK ≤1d, MEDIUM ≤3d, LOW >3d).
  • Official TSDC Notice Board: Digitally verified circulars with reference numbers, action cutoffs, and 1-click Pin-to-Agenda.
  • Multimodal AI Study Copilot: Speech-to-text natural language task creator understanding Hinglish & English voice input.
  • Local-First Resilience: 100% operational offline without crashing during college Wi-Fi blackouts.
Point 5 Proposed Solution & Methodology Local-First Architecture
🗣️ What to speak: "We adopted a Local-First Event-Driven Methodology. The client stores and calculates risk metrics locally via TanStack Query and localStorage for instantaneous sub-second response times, then synchronizes state seamlessly with PostgreSQL Supabase backend when online."

Point 8 System Design, Architecture & Mathematical Formula Engine Logic
⚙️ Mathematical Deadline Risk Calculation Formula: Δt = Math.ceil((Deadline_Midnight - Today_Midnight) / 86400000)
• If status is 'Completed' → DONE
• If pending & Δt < 0 → OVERDUE (Urgent Action Required)
• If pending & Δt ≤ 1 → HIGH RISK (≤ 24 Hours Alert)
• If pending & Δt ≤ 3 → MEDIUM RISK (≤ 3 Days Warning)
• If pending & Δt > 3 → LOW RISK (On Schedule)
